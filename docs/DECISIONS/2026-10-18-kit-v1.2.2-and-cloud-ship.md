## M1 subtitle replacement presentation (Owner revised, 2026-10-18)

**Superseding Owner decision:** The solid-black subtitle plate must adapt to the NEW English subtitle length, not stay limited to the original Chinese glyph width. For each cue, measure the original source Chinese glyph rectangle and the actual rendered English glyph/outline rectangle, then cover their **tight union** with only small glyph-edge/outline padding. This supersedes earlier source-width-only constraints in this decision, the WP and historical reports. The simple output remains: black over old subtitles and new subtitles over the black.

- English remains anchored at the source caption position with **equivalent apparent glyph height**; do not silently shrink or enlarge to bypass acceptance. For longer English, lay out balanced lines within the safe subtitle zone, measuring the actual glyph rectangles before sizing the mask. Translation meaning must not be truncated to force PASS.
- The dynamic black plate may grow horizontally with English text and vertically only as necessary for valid line breaks. It must stay as small as the union actually requires, not become a fixed full-width/lower-third band. It appears only within the original cue time, not during gaps. Preserve original audio, logo, AI badge and surrounding imagery.
- If neither English layout nor its union mask can fit the safe frame while avoiding all protected regions, or source/English geometry is unconfirmed, mark NEEDS_REVIEW or SUBTITLE_REGION_UNCONFIRMED with measured evidence. Never falsely PASS by cropping, deleting meaning, shrinking font, hiding glyphs or encroaching on protected ROIs. No inpainting, blur, delogo or crop/zoom.
- **Acceptance A3:** audit all cues for Chinese glyph bbox and complete English glyph/outline bbox contained by the minimal black plate, expected font equivalence, small padding, safe screen placement, protected ROI clearance and no mask in gaps. Inspect cue_002 and formerly blocked cue_034/035/037/038 plus frames 10.5/15/83.1/86.7/115 seconds. Only zero blocking layouts and real artifact/provenance evidence can support M1 Product PASS.
